How to play

Every episode has three logic questions: Round 1, Round 2 and The Final. No general knowledge needed, just clear thinking.

Each question has 60 seconds on the clock. Every second left is £1 in the bank, so the faster you get it, the more you win.

You've got three buzzers on your podium for the whole show. A wrong answer uses one up, but you can keep trying until the clock runs out. Use all three and the show's over.

Stuck? With 30 seconds left, ask the host for a nudge.
